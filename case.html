  tainted-first: each payment out of an account carries traced money first, then untraced money. Follows the most money outward; can overstate the traced share downstream when a mule also holds other funds.
  clean-first: untraced money leaves first and traced money stays in each account for as long as possible. The most conservative view of how far the money travelled.
  fifo: money leaves each account in the order it arrived (first in, first out), as in the rule in Clayton's Case.
  prorata: every payment carries traced and untraced money in proportion to the account's balance at that moment (a rateable, pari passu split).
